Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the broad science of building computer systems that can mimic human intelligence, reasoning, and problem-solving, while Machine Learning (ML) is a core subset of AI that trains algorithms on data to learn patterns and make decisions autonomously without hard-coded rules.
An overarching field that includes logic rules, expert systems, robotics, natural language processing (NLP), and machine learning.
A specific approach within AI where systems use statistical models to learn from historical data rather than following static instructions.
